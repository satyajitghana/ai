fig1.png is a chart drawn by Artificial Analysis (artificialanalysis.ai) and
published by inclusionAI in the model card; the ratings in it are Artificial
Analysis's.

Modifications. Every figure was converted to RGB on a white background and
resized to at most 1600 px wide (fig2.jpg to 1400 px), and the photographic
ones re-encoded as JPEG. fig4.jpg rearranges the source's single row of eight
panels into two rows of four; no panel was altered. fig7.png is my own
three-panel rendering of one RGBA sample: the image composited onto a
checkerboard I drew, its alpha channel alone as greyscale, and its RGB channels
with the alpha discarded. The source's pixels are otherwise unchanged.

No video was published with this release, on the Hub or on GitHub, so none is
reproduced here.

Everything else in the article's interactive figures is my own work, built
from numbers I read from the repositories and from Artificial Analysis.
