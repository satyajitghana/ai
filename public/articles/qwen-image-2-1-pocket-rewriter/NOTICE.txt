Each sheet shows four images generated by Qwen-Image-2.1 for one request: from the
raw request, and from the rewrites of Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1-PE-T2I and of the two
ML-Intern-lab pocket rewriters. The images are outputs of Qwen Materials and the
dataset is distributed under the Qwen Research License Agreement, a copy of which
is in this directory as QWEN-RESEARCH-LICENSE.txt. The attribution notice that
Agreement requires in Section 3(c):

  Hangzhou Tongyi Laboratory Technology Co., Ltd. All Rights Reserved.

Built with Qwen. Non-commercial research and evaluation use only.

Modifications (Section 3(b)): each sheet was composited onto white, resized from
1720 to 1600 pixels wide, and reduced to a 256-colour palette to keep the page
light. Nothing was cropped, added, relabelled or altered. The tile labels (raw,
teacher, student08, student2b) are the dataset's own.

Everything else on the page — the diagrams, the tables and the arithmetic — is my
own work.
