branch-loop.{webm,mp4} and branch-loop-poster.jpg are a 10.5-second excerpt
(from 59.5 s to 70.0 s) of assets/xgen-jing-demo.mp4 in XGEN-JING
(https://github.com/XGEN-Labs/XGEN-JING), at commit cbeec31. The excerpt was
scaled to 1280 px wide, re-encoded to VP9 and H.264, and its audio track was
dropped so the clip can autoplay silently.

License Agreement, a copy of which is in this directory as
MINIMAX-H3-COMMUNITY-LICENSE.txt. The repository's own third-party attribution
file is beside it as XGEN-JING-NOTICE.txt. Both travel with the excerpt.
